# What Katie Seas Shop Return Policy

Version: returns-v5-2026-08-14  
Effective date: August 14, 2026

Every shirt is printed just for you after ordering. That keeps the shop low-waste, but it also means sizing choices are final sale unless something arrives damaged, defective, or wrong. The exact claim windows, evidence rules, and shipping issue rules depend on the provider that makes each item. Product pages and the cart show a short summary first, with the full provider-specific terms available before checkout; those terms are also saved with your order and included in your confirmation email.

## If something arrives damaged, defective, or wrong

Please email me as soon as you notice the issue. If your item arrives damaged, has a printing defect, or you received the wrong item, color, size, or design, I’ll help make it right.

Each product page and cart item shows the provider-specific claim window and evidence requirements for that item before checkout. Some providers publish a hard maximum claim window and a shorter recommended reporting target; when both exist, the shorter target is a recommendation to help resolve the issue faster, not a general return deadline.

To move quickly, please include your order number, a short description of what happened, and clear photos if the issue can be shown visually. If the provider does not publish a required photo checklist, I may still ask for photos operationally so I can explain the issue clearly.

## Size changes and buyer’s remorse

Because each shirt is made to order, size changes, color changes, and buyer’s remorse are **final sale** when the item was made correctly.

That means I can’t offer free returns or exchanges if the size, color, or design you chose is correct but you changed your mind or it doesn’t fit the way you hoped. If a provider publishes a long window for requesting an eligible return, that only applies to returns that are already eligible or approved. It is not a general “return anything for any reason” window.

Please check the size guide on the product page before ordering. It shows the garment measurements and the supplier’s tolerance, so you can compare them with a shirt you already like.

If the garment you receive is outside the published garment measurements by more than the stated tolerance, that is treated as a defect and is covered. What is not covered is choosing a size that was made correctly but does not fit the way you hoped.

## Wrong shipping address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email me right away.

If the provider publishes an address-change cutoff, that cutoff is shown before checkout and saved with the order. If the provider does not publish a cutoff, I cannot promise that an address can be changed after checkout. I’ll still try to help quickly, but made-to-order providers may start processing before an address change is possible.

If a package is returned or lost because the customer entered an incorrect or incomplete address, the provider-specific reshipping rule shown for that item applies. If the provider does not publish a reshipping rule, I cannot promise a free reshipment.

## Lost packages

If your package seems lost in transit and tracking has stopped updating, contact me. I’ll help check the shipment and file a claim if needed.

Lost-package claim windows vary by provider. The window and the date it is counted from are shown for each item before checkout and saved with your order.

If tracking says the package was delivered but you can’t find it, please first check around the delivery location, ask neighbors/front desk/housemates, and contact the local carrier. The provider-specific delivered-but-not-received policy shown for your item controls whether a free replacement or refund is available, but I’ll still help you figure out the best next step.

## Production and shipping timing

Each product page shows the provider’s production timing. If the provider publishes a transit estimate, that estimate is shown before checkout. If the provider does not publish a transit estimate, I won’t invent one; you’ll receive tracking when the order ships.

## Refund timing

If a refund is approved, it may take a few business days to show up on your original payment method depending on your bank or card issuer.

## Need help?

Email me at the shop support address listed in your order confirmation. I’ll always try to handle issues fairly and personally — I just need the policy to match how made-to-order printing actually works.
