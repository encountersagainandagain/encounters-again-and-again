# What Katie Seas Shop Returns, Exchanges, and Order Issues

Version: returns-v1-2026-08-09  
Effective: August 9, 2026

Every shirt is printed just for you after ordering. That keeps the shop low-waste, but it also means sizing choices are final sale unless something arrives damaged, defective, or wrong.

## If something arrives damaged, defective, or wrong

Please email me as soon as you notice the issue. If your item arrives damaged, has a printing defect, or you received the wrong item, color, size, or design, I’ll help make it right.

To move quickly, please include:

- your order number;
- a short description of what happened;
- clear photos of the issue;
- for print placement or size issues, a photo of the full shirt and the size tag if relevant;
- for a wrong item, a photo of the product and packing slip if you have it.

These details are required because the shirts are fulfilled by a print-on-demand partner, and they need photo evidence before approving a replacement or refund.

Please contact me within 30 days of receiving your order so there’s enough time to file the claim.

## Size changes and buyer’s remorse

Because each shirt is made to order, size changes, color changes, and buyer’s remorse are final sale when the item was made correctly.

That means I can’t offer free returns or exchanges if the size, color, or design you chose is correct but you changed your mind or it doesn’t fit the way you hoped.

Please check the size guide on the product page before ordering. It shows the garment measurements and the supplier’s tolerance, so you can compare them with a shirt you already like.

If the garment you receive is outside the published garment measurements by more than the stated tolerance, that is treated as a defect and is covered. What is not covered is choosing a size that was made correctly but does not fit the way you hoped.

If you’re between sizes, I’d rather you ask before ordering than end up with a shirt you don’t love.

## Wrong shipping address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email me right away. I may be able to update it if the order has not shipped yet.

Once the package has shipped, the address usually can’t be changed. If it gets returned because the address was incorrect or incomplete, I’ll contact you about the next step, but reshipping may require an additional shipping charge.

## Lost packages

If your package seems lost in transit and tracking has stopped updating, contact me. I’ll help check the shipment and file a claim if needed.

Claims for packages lost in transit need to be reported within 30 days of the estimated delivery date.

If tracking says the package was delivered but you can’t find it, please first check around the delivery location, ask neighbors/front desk/housemates, and contact the local carrier. In that specific case, the print partner usually does not cover a free replacement because the carrier marked it delivered, but I’ll still help you figure out the best next step.

## Refund timing

If a refund is approved, it may take a few business days to show up on your original payment method depending on your bank or card issuer.

## Need help?

Email me at the shop support address listed in your order confirmation. I’ll always try to handle issues fairly and personally — I just need the policy to match how made-to-order printing actually works.
