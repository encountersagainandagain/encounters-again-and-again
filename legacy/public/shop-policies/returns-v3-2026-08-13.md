# What Katie Seas Shop Returns, Exchanges, and Order Issues

Version: returns-v3-2026-08-13  
Effective: August 13, 2026

Every shirt is printed just for you after ordering. That keeps the shop low-waste, but it also means sizing choices are final sale unless something arrives damaged, defective, or wrong. The exact claim windows and evidence requirements depend on the provider that makes each item, and those provider-specific terms are shown on the product page, in the cart, and in your order confirmation.

## If something arrives damaged, defective, or wrong

Please email me as soon as you notice the issue. If your item arrives damaged, has a printing defect, or you received the wrong item, color, size, or design, I’ll help make it right.

Each product page lists the provider-specific claim window and evidence requirements for that item. Those are the rules I have to follow when I open a claim with the provider, and the same provider terms are saved with your order.

To move quickly, please include:

- your order number;
- a short description of what happened;
- clear photos of the issue;
- for print placement or size issues, a photo of the full shirt and the size tag if relevant;
- for a wrong item, a photo of the product and packing slip if you have it.

These details are required because the shirts are fulfilled by print-on-demand providers, and the provider may need photo evidence before approving a replacement or refund. Please contact me within the provider-specific claim window shown for your item so there is enough time to file the claim.

## Size changes and buyer’s remorse

Because each shirt is made to order, size changes, color changes, and buyer’s remorse are final sale when the item was made correctly.

That means I can’t offer free returns or exchanges if the size, color, or design you chose is correct but you changed your mind or it doesn’t fit the way you hoped.

Please check the size guide on the product page before ordering. It shows the garment measurements and the supplier’s tolerance, so you can compare them with a shirt you already like.

If the garment you receive is outside the published garment measurements by more than the stated tolerance, that is treated as a defect and is covered. What is not covered is choosing a size that was made correctly but does not fit the way you hoped.

If you’re between sizes, I’d rather you ask before ordering than end up with a shirt you don’t love.

## Wrong shipping address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email me right away. Each provider has its own cutoff for address corrections; the cutoff for your item is shown before checkout and saved with the order.

Once the provider can no longer change the address, the package may have to continue to the address entered at checkout. If it gets returned because the address was incorrect or incomplete, the provider-specific reshipping rule shown for that item applies.

## Lost packages

If your package seems lost in transit and tracking has stopped updating, contact me. I’ll help check the shipment and file a claim if needed.

Lost-package claim windows vary by provider. The window and the date it is counted from are shown for each item before checkout and saved with your order.

If tracking says the package was delivered but you can’t find it, please first check around the delivery location, ask neighbors/front desk/housemates, and contact the local carrier. The provider-specific delivered-but-not-received policy shown for your item controls whether a free replacement or refund is available, but I’ll still help you figure out the best next step.

## Refund timing

If a refund is approved, it may take a few business days to show up on your original payment method depending on your bank or card issuer.
