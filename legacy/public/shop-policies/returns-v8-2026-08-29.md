# What Katie Seas Shop Return Policy

Version: returns-v8-2026-08-29  
Effective date: August 29, 2026

Every shirt is printed just for you after ordering. That keeps the shop low-waste, but it also means sizing choices are final sale once production starts unless something arrives damaged, defective, wrong, or outside the published garment tolerance. If you catch a mistake before production starts, email immediately with your order number so I can try to cancel and refund before the shirt is made.

## Quick summary

- Covered: damage, defects, wrong items, wrong print/color, lost-package claims when the published policy covers them, and garments outside the published measurement tolerance.
- Not covered after production starts: size changes, color changes, buyer’s remorse, or preference changes when the item was made correctly.
- Cancellation: email immediately with your order number. If production has not started, I can try to cancel and issue a full refund.
- Timing: the applicable claim windows are shown on your product, cart, and confirmation email. Please report issues as soon as possible.
- Returns: do not mail anything back unless I first confirm that a return is approved and give you the correct instructions.
- Refunds: approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 7 business days; your bank or card issuer may take several more business days to display the money.

## Canceling before production starts

If you ordered the wrong size, color, design, or shipping address, email me immediately with your order number. If production has not started yet, I can try to cancel the order and issue a full refund.

This window can be short because made-to-order production often begins within 1–3 business days. Once production starts, the item is being made specifically for you and size, color, or buyer’s remorse changes are no longer cancelable.

I’ll always be honest about where the order is in the process. If it can still be stopped cleanly, I’d rather fix it that way than have you stuck with something you caught right away.

## If something arrives damaged, defective, or wrong

Please email me as soon as you notice the issue. If your item arrives damaged, has a printing defect, or you received the wrong item, color, size, or design, I’ll help make it right.

Each product page and cart item shows the applicable claim window and evidence requirements before checkout. When both a hard maximum and a shorter recommended reporting target are shown, the shorter target is a recommendation to help resolve the issue faster, not a general return deadline.

To move quickly, please include your order number, a short description of what happened, and clear photos of the issue. For print placement or size issues, include a photo of the full shirt and the size tag if relevant. For a wrong item, include a photo of the product and packing slip if you have it.

These details let me assess and document the issue accurately. Even when a specific photo checklist is not shown, I may still ask for photos so I can resolve the claim fairly and quickly.

## Size changes and buyer’s remorse

Because each shirt is made to order, size changes, color changes, and buyer’s remorse are **final sale** when the item was made correctly.

The reason is practical, not personal: each garment is printed for the person who ordered it, so a correctly made shirt usually cannot be put back into stock and sold to someone else.

That means I can’t offer free returns or exchanges after production starts if the size, color, or design you chose is correct but you changed your mind or it doesn’t fit the way you hoped.

If a longer window is shown for requesting an eligible return, it only applies to returns that are already eligible or approved. It is not a general “return anything for any reason” window.

Please check the size guide on the product page before ordering. It shows the garment measurements and measurement tolerance, so you can compare them with a shirt you already like.

If the garment you receive is outside the published garment measurements by more than the stated tolerance, that is treated as a defect and is covered. What is not covered is choosing a size that was made correctly but does not fit the way you hoped.

If you’re between sizes, I’d rather you ask before ordering than end up with a shirt you don’t love.

## Wrong shipping address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email me right away.

When an address-change cutoff applies, it is shown before checkout and saved with the order. If no cutoff is shown, I cannot promise that an address can be changed after checkout. I’ll still try to help quickly, but made-to-order processing may begin before an address change is possible.

If a package is returned or lost because the customer entered an incorrect or incomplete address, the reshipping rule shown for that item applies. If no free reshipment is stated, I cannot promise one.

## Return authorization

Please do not send anything back unless I first confirm that the return is approved and give you the correct instructions. Unauthorized returns cannot be accepted, and a fixed return address may not apply because returns are handled case by case after a claim is reviewed.

If a return is approved, I’ll explain who pays return shipping based on the terms saved with your order and what happened. If the issue was caused by a customer address mistake or a change of preference, return or reshipping costs may be the customer’s responsibility.

## Production and shipping timing

Each product page shows the estimated production timing. When a transit estimate is available, it is shown before checkout.

If a transit estimate is not available, I won’t invent one; you’ll receive tracking when the order ships.

## Lost packages

If your package seems lost in transit and tracking has stopped updating, contact me. I’ll help check the shipment and file a claim if needed.

The lost-package claim window and the date it is counted from are shown for each item before checkout and saved with your order.

If tracking says the package was delivered but you can’t find it, please first check around the delivery location, ask neighbors/front desk/housemates, and contact the local carrier. The delivered-but-not-received terms shown for your item determine whether a free replacement or refund is available, but I’ll still help you figure out the best next step.

## Refund timing

If a refund is approved, I process it back to the original payment method within 7 business days. After that, your bank or card issuer may take several more business days to show it on your account.

If shipping was charged as a separate line and every item in that package is refunded, the remaining shipping balance for that package is refunded too. If only part of a package is refunded and other items are still being sent, shipping is not automatically reduced because that package still needs to move forward.

If an order has already shipped, shipping refunds are handled case by case based on the terms saved with the order and what actually happened. I’ll explain the decision in the refund email so you know what is still being sent and what was returned.

## Customs, duties, and import charges

Some products may ship from an international fulfillment center. If customs, duties, import taxes, carrier brokerage fees, or similar charges are assessed by customs or the carrier, they are the recipient’s responsibility unless checkout explicitly says they were prepaid.

For the first United States-only launch, these charges are not expected to be common, but I do not want to promise that a customs authority or carrier will never assess them.

## Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under applicable law. If a required law gives you stronger rights than this policy, that law controls.

## Need help?

Email me at the shop support address listed in your order confirmation. I’ll always try to handle issues fairly and personally — I just need the policy to match how made-to-order printing actually works.
