Following our nature

Through our encounters in life

We encountered in one of the most extraordinary places on the Earth — the Great Barrier Reef.

In a little patch of sand in the middle of the vast ocean. Among sea turtles, whales and manta rays, we discovered a shared passion for the animals, the ocean, art and for protecting the living world around us.

That encounter brought us together.

A project to unite them all.

Now we have crossed to the other side of Australia, continuing that journey and building Encounters Expeditions.

A project dedicated to supporting conservation, storytelling and a deeper relationship to the ocean and the animals that live within it.
