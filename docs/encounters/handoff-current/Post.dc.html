01 · Leaving late

We left two weeks later than planned

paragraph

The engine needed a part that took eleven days to arrive, which is how we learned that the boat decides the calendar.

We spent that time fixing things we had been ignoring: the bilge pump, the second reef line, the way the fridge drained into the wrong place. By the time we finally motored out of the bay nobody was in a hurry any more, which turned out to be the right way to start.
