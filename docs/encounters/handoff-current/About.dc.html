Our mission

To connect people with nature — and to keep that connection alive long after the encounter is over.

Our stories

We tell them with different art media: filmography and photography, writing and illustration. Whatever gets somebody close enough to an animal or a place to start caring about it.

Our actions

Sea expeditions, beach cleanups, research assistance, animal workshops, conservation and communication efforts. We believe every action, no matter how small, makes a difference.
