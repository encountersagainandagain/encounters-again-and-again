Workshop 01 · Running

Draw the ocean

three sessions
Who it is for

Schools and community groups, no drawing experience needed. We bring the materials and the stories; the room brings the animals it wants to meet.

01
Looking first

We open with photographs and the stories behind them — what it felt like to be in the water with a whale, what the turtle was doing before the camera came out. Nobody draws yet.

02
Drawing together

Georgia works through her own process out loud — the shapes she starts with, the mistakes she keeps. Everyone picks one animal and stays with it until the page is full.

03
Putting it up

Every drawing goes on the wall, with the name of the animal and one line about why it was chosen. The room reads itself, and the wall stays after we leave.

Workshop 02 · Running

Encounters at the market

two sessions
A note about the stall

It travels: a table, the book, the prints and whatever we can carry. Half of it is selling, half of it is talking to whoever stops.

01
The stall

We set up early with the book, the prints and a stack of blank paper. Anyone who stops gets asked the same question: which animal have you actually met?

02
The reading

Late in the afternoon we read a few pages out loud for whoever is around, kids sitting on the grass. It usually turns into a conversation about the sea instead.

Workshop 03 · Finished

Wild wonders at Coledale

two afternoons
What stayed

Forty-one drawings, a wall that stayed up for a month, and three families who came back for the second afternoon.

—
How it went

Two afternoons at the markets, one long table, and more people than chairs. Everyone drew the animal they had met — and a few who had never met one drew the one they wanted to.
